08. Brand Rules & Misuse (Do's & Don'ts)

Strict guidelines to preserve visual integrity. Never alter the geometry or styling of the master files.

Classification Rule Description Reasoning
DO Always use official SVGs from /public/brand/ or the <BrandLogo /> React component. Guarantees crisp subpixel rendering, zero layout shift, and correct geometry.
DO Switch to the inverted white logo whenever placing on backgrounds darker than 40% luminance. Ensures high-contrast accessibility compliance (WCAG AAA).
DON'T Never stretch, squash, or distort the aspect ratio of the symbol or wordmark. Destroys the architectural proportions and 60-degree angle.
DON'T Never add drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, or gradients directly to the vector mark. Dilutes the sober, editorial command-center agency aesthetic.
DON'T Never rotate the mark or change the relative scale between symbol and typography. Weakens trademark memorability and brand recall.
DON'T Never recolor the logo with unapproved colors outside the brand palette. Brand equity requires consistency of concept and palette discipline.
